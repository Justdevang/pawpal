## 1. Introduction

Welcome to PawPal ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application PawPal (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
- **Account Data**: Email address, name, authentication tokens (Google/Apple Sign-In)
- **Profile Data**: Profile photo, preferred language, units (metric/imperial), theme preference

### 2.2 Pet Information
- **Pet Profiles**: Name, species, breed, sex, birth date/approximate age, weight, microchip number, photos, notes
- **Health Records**: Vaccination records (name, date, next due, vet clinic, batch number, certificate photos), medication records (name, dosage, form, frequency, schedule, vet info), weight entries, vet appointments, documents

### 2.3 Usage Data
- **Analytics**: App opens, feature usage, screen views, error reports
- **Device Info**: Device model, OS version, app version, unique device identifier
- **Notifications**: Push notification tokens, delivery/read receipts

### 2.4 Family Sharing Data
- **Invitations**: Email addresses of invited family members
- **Permissions**: Role assignments (Owner/Editor/Viewer), acceptance status

- Comply with legal obligations

### 4.1 With Your Consent

## 5. Data Storage & Security

### 5.1 Storage Locations
- **Local-First**: All data stored on your device first (SQLite via Drift)
- **Cloud Sync**: Encrypted sync to Firebase Firestore when online
- **Backups**: Encrypted backups to Firebase Storage

### 5.2 Encryption
- **In Transit**: TLS 1.2+ for all network communication
- **At Rest**: AES-256 encryption for cloud data
- **Local**: Platform secure storage (Keychain/Keystore) for tokens

### 5.3 Access Controls
- Authentication required for all data access
- Role-based permissions for family sharing
- Biometric/Face ID optional app lock

---

## 6. Your Rights & Choices

### 6.1 Access & Portability
- View all your data in the App
- Export all data as JSON/CSV/PDF anytime
- Request a complete data copy

### 6.2 Correction & Deletion
- Edit any pet or health record
- Delete individual records or entire pets
- **Delete Account**: Permanently removes all your data from our servers within 30 days

### 6.3 Opt-Out Controls
- **Push Notifications**: Disable in device settings or App settings
- **Analytics**: Disable in App settings (Settings → Data & Privacy)
- **Cloud Sync**: Disable for local-only mode
- **Marketing Emails**: Unsubscribe link in every email

### 6.4 Regional Rights
- **GDPR (EU)**: Right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, object
- **CCPA (California)**: Right to know, delete, opt-out of sale, non-discrimination
- **LGPD (Brazil)**: Similar rights under Brazilian law
- **DPDP (India)**: Digital Personal Data Protection Act compliance

---

## 6.5 Children's Privacy (COPPA)

- We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13
